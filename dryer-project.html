Executive Summary

Post-harvest losses remain a critical bottleneck in agricultural supply chains, heavily driven by unpredictable weather conditions and inadequate traditional drying techniques. This project introduces an advanced hybrid biomass-solar aided agricultural dryer integrated with a zeolite-based moisture regulation system to ensure continuous, high-efficiency crop dehydration regardless of ambient climate volatility.

Simulation & Flow Modeling

Computational fluid dynamics and thermal distribution mapping were executed to analyze airflow behavior, temperature gradients, and moisture extraction rates inside the drying chamber.

Design Objectives

  • Thermal Efficiency: Maximize solar thermal gain during peak daylight hours while utilizing a biomass backup burner for nocturnal or overcast operations.
  • Precision Adsorption: Implement zeolite desiccants to capture and regulate chamber humidity, preventing overdrying or localized crop spoilage.
  • Structural & Airflow Optimization: Design internal baffles and plenum chambers using CAD/CAE tools to guarantee uniform velocity profiles across all product trays.
