About Me

I’m a Registered Mechanical Engineer specializing in CAD/CAE modeling, thermodynamic analysis, HVAC & power system design, and industrial maintenance.

I turn technical challenges and operational data into efficient engineering solutions that help organizations optimize thermal systems, improve equipment reliability, and make better design decisions. My experience includes drafting with AutoCAD and SolidWorks, costing Bills of Materials (BOM), performing CFD and motion analysis, and executing maintenance for mission-critical units.

I take an end-to-end approach to engineering—from understanding the operational problem and scoping requirements to design, simulation, prototyping, and final implementation.

I’m open to part-time, project-based, freelance, consulting, and full-time opportunities in Mechanical Design, HVAC Engineering, Thermal Systems, and Project Coordination.

Every project in this portfolio follows a structured engineering workflow—from defining the technical requirements to delivering optimized, data-backed solutions.
